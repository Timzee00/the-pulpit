Privacy & AI limits

Updated October 5, 2026.

What is sent

AI requests send your message and recent conversation context through The Pulpit to Groq or OpenRouter and its selected provider. Sermon translation may use Microsoft Translator; configured voice reading uses ElevenLabs. Research queries go to public reference services. These services apply their own retention policies. Avoid passwords, financial identifiers, or details that identify another person.

What stays on your device

Versiah conversations are saved only if you enable saving. Turning saving off removes the locally saved conversation. New conversation clears the current history. An exported text file is your responsibility to store securely. Sermon history, bookmarks and Bible notes use browser storage. Shared devices can expose saved material.

Access protection

Cloudflare Turnstile verifies visitors. A signed, HttpOnly guest-access cookie lasts 15 minutes. Hashed network identifiers and aggregate request counts are kept in Redis for up to two days to limit abuse; retrieved public Scripture passages may be cached for seven days. Conversations are not intentionally stored in Redis. Hosting and upstream services can retain request metadata independently.

AI boundaries

Retrieved Scripture text is English WEB. Translated explanations and study plans are AI-generated, may be inaccurate, and are not divine revelation. Interpretations differ across Christian traditions. Check the passage in context and seek appropriate pastoral support. Versiah is not an emergency service, counsellor, doctor or lawyer.

If you are in immediate danger or might hurt yourself, contact local emergency services or the nearest emergency department and someone you trust now. Do not wait for an AI answer.

Service limits

Public use has request limits and may be temporarily unavailable. No response should be treated as a promise of health, wealth, divine instruction, or guaranteed spiritual outcomes.
